About Us

Technology deserves care, not unnecessary replacement.

At The Computer Care Company, we believe that a computer problem should begin with a proper diagnosis, not an unnecessary purchase. Our focus is on extending the life of your devices through reliable repairs, careful maintenance, and practical technical support.

We provide computer and laptop repairs, software installation and troubleshooting, PC builds, printer services, and CCTV solutions. Whether it is a minor software issue or a complex hardware fault, our approach remains the same: identify the actual problem, explain the available solutions, and help you make an informed decision.

Our Approach

  • 1

    Diagnose Before We Decide.

    We investigate the cause of the problem before recommending a solution.

  • 2

    Explain Before We Proceed.

    We communicate our findings, available options, and expected costs so you can decide with confidence.

  • 3

    Repair Before Replacing.

    Whenever practical, we prioritise repair and reuse over unnecessary replacement and spending.

  • 4

    Test Before We Return.

    We carry out appropriate checks to help ensure your device is functioning as expected before handover.

Built on Trust and Transparency

We understand that your devices are an important part of your work, education, business, and everyday life. That is why we value clear communication, responsible handling, and service you can understand without technical confusion.

Our goal is not simply to fix a device, but to help you get the most out of the technology you already own.

"The Computer Care Company — Complete care. Complete support. Technology that lasts."
