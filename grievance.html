Grievance & Redressal

“Our aim is to resolve genuine concerns fairly and respectfully, without unnecessary inconvenience to the customer”

How our grievance & redressal process works

How to Raise a Grievance

  • Contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email, or in person at the relevant branch.
  • Please provide your service/job reference, invoice details, or other information that helps us identify the service.
  • Briefly describe the concern and, where possible, the outcome you believe would resolve it.
  • We will review the matter, discuss it with the relevant team member where necessary, and communicate the outcome to you.

Our Redressal Approach

  • We will listen to your concern without dismissing it.
  • We will review the service records, diagnosis, work performed, parts used, and applicable warranty terms where relevant.
  • If we find that we have made an error or failed to meet our stated service commitments, we will explain the corrective action available.
  • Where a requested remedy is not covered by the service or warranty terms, we will explain the reason clearly.

Grievances vs Warranty

A grievance does not automatically extend, replace, or alter the terms of our stated 60-day service warranty. Warranty eligibility and exclusions will continue to be assessed according to the service performed and the applicable warranty terms.

Escalation

If your concern is not resolved at the first point of contact, you may request that it be escalated to the person responsible for customer service or management at the relevant branch.
