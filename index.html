Repair Before Replacement

"We Don't Sell You a Replacement Just Because You Have a Problem"

We believe a problem does not always mean a device needs to be replaced. We diagnose the problem first, explain the available options, and recommend the most practical solution based on your needs and budget. Whenever repair is a reliable option, we believe in repairing and extending the life of your equipment rather than encouraging unnecessary replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common concerns before you book a service.

Do I need to back up my data before giving you my computer?
While we handle customer equipment responsibly and prioritize data safety, we highly encourage customers to maintain their own backups before handing over any device to prevent unforeseen loss during hardware failure.
Will you format my computer during repair?
No. A software problem does not always require formatting. We attempt to resolve the actual cause without unnecessarily erasing your applications, settings, or data. If a format is unavoidable, we require your explicit approval first.
Will I be informed before replacing any parts?
Absolutely. We explain what needs to be repaired or replaced, estimated costs, and options (New/OEM/refurbished) before doing any major work. You always have the final say.
How much does diagnosis cost?
We provide a free initial diagnosis for eligible computer and equipment problems. You pay no fees just for a check-up.

Grievance & Redressal

“Our aim is to resolve genuine concerns fairly and respectfully, without unnecessary inconvenience to the customer”

How our grievance & redressal process works

How to Raise a Grievance

  • Contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email, or in person at the relevant branch.
  • Please provide your service/job reference, invoice details, or other information that helps us identify the service.
  • Briefly describe the concern and, where possible, the outcome you believe would resolve it.
  • We will review the matter, discuss it with the relevant team member where necessary, and communicate the outcome to you.

Our Redressal Approach

  • We will listen to your concern without dismissing it.
  • We will review the service records, diagnosis, work performed, parts used, and applicable warranty terms where relevant.
  • If we find that we have made an error or failed to meet our stated service commitments, we will explain the corrective action available.
  • Where a requested remedy is not covered by the service or warranty terms, we will explain the reason clearly.

Grievances vs Warranty

A grievance does not automatically extend, replace, or alter the terms of our stated 60-day service warranty. Warranty eligibility and exclusions will continue to be assessed according to the service performed and the applicable warranty terms.

Escalation

If your concern is not resolved at the first point of contact, you may request that it be escalated to the person responsible for customer service or management at the relevant branch.
