Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common concerns before you book a service.

Do I need to back up my data before giving you my computer?
While we handle customer equipment responsibly and prioritize data safety, we highly encourage customers to maintain their own backups before handing over any device to prevent unforeseen loss during hardware failure.
Will you format my computer during repair?
No. A software problem does not always require formatting. We attempt to resolve the actual cause without unnecessarily erasing your applications, settings, or data. If a format is unavoidable, we require your explicit approval first.
Will I be informed before replacing any parts?
Absolutely. We explain what needs to be repaired or replaced, estimated costs, and options (New/OEM/refurbished) before doing any major work. You always have the final say.
How much does diagnosis cost?
We provide a free initial diagnosis for eligible computer and equipment problems. You pay no fees just for a check-up.
